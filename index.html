01THE PREMISE

THE PROBLEM

You shouldn't need to risk a spoiler to remember a name.

Ordinary search treats every chapter as fair game. Readers do not. A character's significance can change, a relationship can turn, and even a harmless summary can ruin a reveal.

This project began with a different unit of information: the last completed chapter or episode. Everything downstream—the shelf, character notes, evidence search and optional AI—has to respect that boundary.

“

Design principle
The software should be able to say “I don't know” and point to the page it does know.

01 / PRODUCT THINKING

Uncertainty is a feature.

Names that may refer to the same character are flagged for review rather than merged on a guess. Family ties and direct interactions remain distinct evidence types.

02 / PRIVACY

Keep the story private.

Imported books and local model services stay on the owner's host. This portfolio page runs entirely in the browser and contains no imported novels or personal accounts.

03 / RELIABILITY

Tests over tall promises.

The C110 checkpoint reported 1,041 passing automated tests. That is engineering coverage, not a claim of independently verified whole-novel comprehension.
